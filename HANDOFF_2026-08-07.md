## The new blocker: Gemini free-tier quota

The brief endpoint now hits the real Gemini API and gets:
```
[429 Too Many Requests] You exceeded your current quota
* Quota exceeded for metric: generativelanguage.googleapis.com/generate_content_free_tier_input_token_count, limit: 0
* Quota exceeded for metric: generativelanguage.googleapis.com/generate_content_free_tier_requests, limit: 0
Please retry in 22.3s.
```

This is the **free tier** (`generate_content_free_tier_*`) being exhausted. The free tier for `gemini-2.0-flash-lite` allows:
- ~15 requests per minute
- ~1,500 requests per day

The limit: 0 means we've used all 1,500 for today.

### Three options to unblock:

**Option A: Wait for daily reset (free, 0 min work)**
- Quota resets at midnight Pacific Time
- Tomorrow morning the endpoint will work again
- Good enough for low-traffic demo use

**Option B: Switch to a paid Gemini tier (5 min, costs money)**
- Enable billing in Google AI Studio: https://aistudio.google.com/apikey
- Even $5 of credit is enough for thousands of brief generations
- No code changes needed — just add a payment method

**Option C: Switch to a different free model (5 min, no cost)**
- Change `GEMINI_FLASH_L` in `api/_lib/gemini.ts` from `gemini-2.0-flash-lite` to another model
- Examples: `gemini-2.0-flash` (more expensive, separate quota), `gemini-1.5-flash` (older, separate quota)
- Each model has its own quota, so switching gives fresh quota

**My recommendation:** Option B (enable billing). $5 of credit covers months of demo traffic. Otherwise the brief endpoint will keep hitting quota during any active demo.
