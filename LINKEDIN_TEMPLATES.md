- **Template 1-3**: Use for the launch post. Pick one language or post all three on separate days.

- **Template 5**: Use for thought leadership / founder branding posts.
- **Template 6**: Use in tech groups, Build in Public communities, Hacker News threads.
- Always add a relevant image or screenshot of the dashboard when posting.
- Best posting times: 8-10am EST (Tue-Thu) for US audience; 9-11am BRT for LATAM audience.
